1. **👶 [Puer Aeternus (Man Child)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Puer_aeternus)**

My brain has a strong tendency to avoid committing to anything ***“real”*** because of my ego.

From the Wikipedia page:
    
    > *"For the time being one is doing this or that... it is not yet what is really wanted, and there is always the fantasy that sometime in the future the real thing will come about.... The one thing dreaded throughout by such a type of man is to be **bound to anything whatever.”***
    > 
    
    <aside>
    💡
    
    I have a strong fear that I “ruined / missed” my adolescence because of the ED, so I’m still extremely attached to an idealized version of being a child.
    
    I often “deny reality” because of how strong my regret is. Literally *“this is not my life, this is not happening, the good timeline still exists somewhere”*
    
    </aside>
    

1. **💭 [Maladaptive Daydreaming](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maladaptive_daydreaming)**
    
    I do this a lot, listening to music while imagining cool scenarios. I thought it was harmless entertainment at first, but it’s really having a negative effect on my focus and brain. Trying to cut it out.
    
    It also feeds my idealism, instead of dealing with the present moment.
    
2. **🧠 Self Rumination**
    
    I don’t know if you can tell by now… but I take myself way too seriously.
    
    I have a very strong awareness that this is my one & only life, but I’ve been running away from it because of how strong that feeling is.
    
    <aside>
    👉
    
    Core limiting beliefs: 
    
    **- I need to “achieve” greatness** before I can become a real person
        - Causes a lot of delaying
        - You talked about this in your Graham Weaver interview
    
    - **I’m already too broken** to be the person I was supposed to be
        - Haven’t been the person I wanted to be, so far
        - Didn’t go to HYPSM
        - Bill Gates didn’t waste his teenage years to an ED, etc.
    
    </aside>
    

1.  **👎 Negative Thought Loops**
I have a hard time envisioning a positive future for myself, and assume that everyone always hates me / does not want me to show up. 

This is very deep in the subconscious — so don’t worry, I’m fine on a daily basis — but it leads to a lot of self-sabotage and issues.

<aside>
👉

**Every time I try to envision a positive future, my brain goes:**
- “but you didn’t go to Harvard”
- “but you didn’t reach your physical potential because of the ED”
- “but you already messed up your relationship with John/Vitalii by being a man-child”
- “but your whole story is already too messed up”
- “but there are so many people that are better, what’s the point”

</aside>

1. 🌎 **Everything Else on Earth**

Paradoxically, I still have huge ambitions. I want to go further, higher than anyone else has gone before. My inner megalomaniac wants to reach the top.

I still want to become the next **Walt Disney, Steve Jobs, J. Robert Oppenheimer, Yves Saint-Laurent**, etc… all at the same time, which comes back to that idealistic side of myself. This is probably why I was so focused on the “age 19” thing a few years ago.

I want to be visiting the White House, going to the UN, having a say in world events.

… but I’ll need to get the basics down first.

You know the feeling. 

But when I see Trump, Elon, Zohran, etc. **I know I’ll be one of those people,** the ones who change things around. Without getting too egomaniacal, of course.
    
    ![substack.png](attachment:e9b36376-b88d-4a88-8532-60f7efa91451:substack.png)
    

**I think my dream job has yet to be invented.**

Especially in the age of AI, when 1 person will be able to accomplish so much with their life. I’m hugely bullish on AI for that reason, even if most of it feels like slop right now.

**…and I think that’s true for a lot of people. People are losing meaning in the age of AI-automated everything.**

I initially quit Stan to draw a comic, and I did draw it — but **ChatGPT Images** came out afterwards. I felt an incredibly strong feeling of “why waste all of this effort when AI will just do this for me.”

**I want to protect the artisan’s integrity in the age of AI.**
