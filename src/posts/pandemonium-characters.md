Characters!!

Character Sheets are honestly my favorite part of the story creation process *(not the actual story creation... lol),* since they allowe you to creatively think about the distinct markers to give to each character to make them recognizable.

It's especially fun when they're based on pre-existing characters — like in this case, with classical novel characters — since you get to **research + innovate** on the previous depictions of every single character.
